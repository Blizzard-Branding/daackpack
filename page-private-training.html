01 Private Training — You're there for every session
Private Training

You Build the Skills.
We Coach You Through It.

Private sessions are the core follow-up after your consultation. You handle your dog, and your trainer coaches you every step of the way. You are present and actively training at every session — this is a skill-building service for you and your dog together.

  • You work directly with your dog; trainer coaches your handling
  • Focus on 2–3 protocols from your custom consultation plan
  • Held at our Animal Behavior Center, in-home, or virtually
  • Approach adapts as your dog's needs and progress evolve

Each Session

How a Private Session Flows

01

Review Your Plan

Your trainer arrives with your custom plan. You'll revisit where you left off and set the focus for today's session.

02

Train Together

You handle your dog through 2–3 protocols while your trainer coaches your technique and reads your dog's responses.

03

Leave with Tools

Every session ends with practice homework. Skills build on each other and adjust as your dog progresses.

02 Day Training — We lead; you learn the transition
Intensive Support

We Work with Your Dog.
You Learn the Transition.

Day Training is fundamentally different from private sessions. Our trainer works directly with your dog up to 4 times a week — you don't need to be present for most sessions. One dedicated session per week transfers those skills back to you.

  • Trainer does the hands-on work directly with your dog
  • You attend one mandatory transition session per week
  • Weekly written notes after every transition session
  • Webinar access included to support your learning

Standard Program

What a Week Looks Like

Mon / Tue / Wed

Trainer + Your Dog

Your trainer works 1-on-1 with your dog in your home. You don't need to be there — we handle introducing and reinforcing new skills.

Thursday — Required

Transition Session

You join the session. Your trainer walks you through what was taught that week so the skills transfer to you. This is mandatory every week.

End of Week

Weekly Notes

Written notes cover what was taught, your dog's progress, and what to practice before next week.

After 4 Weeks

Wrap-Up

You leave with a trained dog, the skills to maintain progress, and a clear roadmap. Additional weeks can be added if needed.

Attendance encouraged — transition sessions are the only requirement We welcome you at all sessions. The mandatory session each week is the transition — that's where skills transfer back to you. Virtual and Mini programs require client presence at all sessions.
Is Day Training right for you?

Best Suited For

Standard & Mini Day Training

In-Person Cases

  • Leash reactivity, barking, lunging
  • Stranger danger or dog-directed behaviors
  • Obedience-focused or general behavior cases
  • Families with limited time for frequent sessions
  • Complex cases needing high-frequency trainer contact
Virtual Day Training

Remote or Specific Cases

  • Separation anxiety
  • Cooperative care and vet-preparation
  • Owner-directed conflict
  • Out-of-area clients
  • Cases best addressed in the dog's natural environment

All services are valid for 6 months from initial purchase. Sessions are non-refundable and non-cancellable per our waiver. Emergency accommodations handled case-by-case.

Installment Plans

Available for 4 & 8 session packages and all Day Training programs. A 1.5% processing fee applies. $50 late fee for payments more than 24 hours past the invoice date.
