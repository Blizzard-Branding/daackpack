Before You Visit

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a behavior assessment required?

Yes. All dogs must complete an assessment before joining daycare or boarding. This helps us understand your dog's needs and ensure a safe, appropriate environment for everyone.

What vaccinations are required?

Please contact us for current vaccination requirements. We follow veterinary best-practice guidelines to keep all animals in our care safe.

Can I board my dog if they have behavioral challenges?

It depends on the nature and severity of the behavior. Our team is experienced with a wide range of behavioral needs. Contact us to discuss your dog's specific situation and we'll let you know if boarding is appropriate.

What should I bring for boarding?

Please contact us for a full packing checklist. We'll let you know what food, bedding, medications, and other items to bring to make your dog comfortable during their stay.

Do you provide updates during my dog's stay?

Yes. We keep you informed about how your dog is doing. Details about update frequency and communication preferences can be discussed at intake.
