About the Program

A Community Built Around Your Dog

Training is a journey of growth, trust, and connection. Our flexible membership offers a modular curriculum tailored to how you and your dog learn best — from new pet parents to seasoned handlers.

Rooted in positive reinforcement, ethology, behaviorism, and modern science. Inclusive, accessible, and designed to help you and your dog thrive — together.

  • Expert-led by an Applied Animal Behaviorist
  • Great for reactive, fearful, or anxious dogs
  • Real-world excursions in partnership with local businesses
  • Indoor and outdoor locations with low-impact options

Logistics

How Scheduling Works

Monthly Calendar

Once onboarded, you'll receive a monthly calendar with links to select sessions. Classes have a 4–6 dog capacity. If you don't see a link, the class may be full. Updates throughout the month will come with a new calendar.

Monthly Invoicing

Invoices go out the day you sign up and renew on the same date each month. To pause (30 or 60 days), change tiers, or cancel, email bark@daackpack.com at least 2 weeks before your next invoice date.

Getting Started: Group Onboarding Video All new members complete a mandatory 45-minute introductory video covering handling techniques, how to set yourself and your dog up to learn, emergency survival skills, and an introduction to behavior modification.
