The Onboarding
Process

Before any training begins, we learn everything we can about you, your dog, and your household. Good information leads to great results.

Why We Onboard

Comprehensive Information Leads to Better Outcomes

Our onboarding process is thorough by design. Every dog and every case is different — we can't build an effective plan without understanding the full picture. This is true for every case, regardless of complexity.

Our goal is to create a training plan that is inclusive, collaborative, and progress-driven towards your and your animal's specific needs.

  • Understand your dog's full history and behavioral profile
  • Assess your household, lifestyle, and goals
  • Identify the right service pathway for your case
  • Set realistic expectations and a clear training roadmap
