Good to Know

Key Policies

Installment Plans Available for services over $600. Automated payments or scheduled invoices with a 1.5% processing fee.
Rescheduling Policy We have a no-rescheduling policy. Emergency rescheduling with 12+ hours notice: $60 fee per training hour.
Session Validity All services are valid for 6 months from purchase date. Sessions are non-refundable.
Travel Fees Under 15 min: free. Under 30 min: $25. Under 45 min: $50. Under 60 min: $75.
